## disc_flight -- disc position / velocity

Sampled 11 times at 13/14-frame gaps during a live CHALLENGE bout (TRAINING never serves the disc -- see KNOWN_ISSUES.md). Dump range $0000-$40000.

ramdiff's `changed(e,f) changed(f,g)` opener leaves 59 words:

```
./scripts/ramdiff.py --word --range 0x0 0x40000 --max 100000 \
  changed dumps/disc_flight/f.bin dumps/disc_flight/g.bin \
  changed dumps/disc_flight/g.bin dumps/disc_flight/h.bin
```

ramdiff cannot express the interesting part, so analyze.py does it: normalise each interval by its real VBL gap, then keep words that move in *every* interval with a consistent |velocity|. A **sign reversal** separates a bouncing coordinate from a monotone frame counter.

| addr | velocity spread | reversals | values | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `$6ab4` | 0.14 | 0 | 6993, 7007, 7021, 7035, 7049, 7063, 7077, 7091, 7104, 7117, 7131 | monotone at +1/frame and never reverses -- a free-running counter, not a position |
| `$6e3c` | 0.57 | 6 | 14, 20, 6, 12, 18, 4, 10, 16, 3, 10, 17 | |v| ~ 0.6/frame with 6 reversal(s), but confined to 3..20 -- a **wrapping phase counter**, not an integrated coordinate |
| `$6c80` | 0.62 | 6 | -242, -256, -220, -234, -248, -212, -226, -240, -253, -216, -230 | |v| ~ 1.5/frame with 6 reversal(s), but confined to -256..-212 -- a **wrapping phase counter**, not an integrated coordinate |
| `$6c5c` | 0.98 | 4 | 123, 448, 1006, 989, 1008, 785, 771, 82, 396, 470, 507 | |v| ~ 16.4/frame with 4 reversal(s), but confined to 82..1008 -- a **wrapping phase counter**, not an integrated coordinate |
| `$6ce2` | 0.98 | 3 | 29, 15, 1, 5, 39, 25, 11, 3, 2, 1, 48 | |v| ~ 1.1/frame with 3 reversal(s), but confined to 1..48 -- a **wrapping phase counter**, not an integrated coordinate |
| `$6d5c` | 0.99 | 6 | 17910, 17928, 18824, 18072, 18084, 18026, 18044, 17864, 17888, 18166, 18190 | |v| ~ 16.2/frame with 6 reversal(s), but confined to 17864..18824 -- a **wrapping phase counter**, not an integrated coordinate |

**Conclusion (negative result, medium confidence).** No word in $0000-$40000 behaves like an integrated disc coordinate: every word that changes on every frame either advances monotonically at exactly 1/frame (a counter) or stays confined to a small band (a wrapping phase). This scenario already dumps well past $8000, so the disc is not simply stored outside the assumed state area. Either the game recomputes the disc position each frame from a trajectory parameter rather than storing it, or it moves fast enough that a ~14-frame sample aliases it. Next step: shorten the gaps to 1-2 frames, which needs a cheaper dump than a full savebin per sample.

  decreased dumps/tile_hit/pre.bin dumps/tile_hit/t5.bin
